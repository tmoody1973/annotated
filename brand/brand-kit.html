ANNOTATED Brand kit · v1 · 17 Aug 2026

The system

Acid on black, hard edges, no apologies.

Annotated clips a moment out of something someone else published and asks you to say what it means. The identity is a highlighter: one violent yellow laid over black type, with everything drawn as if it were printed rather than rendered. Every value on this page is a live token from apps/web/app/globals.css — the kit is rendered in the system it documents.

01 · The mark

The logo is already the metaphor.

The wordmark is ANNOTATED with an acid block struck over the A — a highlighter stroke, which is the product. The icon is that block on its own. Nothing else needed inventing.

Lockup

ANNOTATED

The wordmark lives on black. On a light ground, invert: black type, acid block unchanged — the acid never changes value.

Clear space

Keep clear space of one third the mark's width on every side. The keyline is part of the mark, not a container — never crop it off.

02 · Icon

It has to survive 16 pixels.

A browser tab is the smallest place this brand appears and the most common. These are the actual shipped files at actual size, not scaled approximations.

The keyline is what keeps the acid square from dissolving into a light tab strip. It is not decoration — drop it and the icon disappears on half of all browsers.

03 · Colour

One accent. It never negotiates.

Acid is the only chromatic value in the system. Everything else is black, paper, or a grey chosen to pass contrast on a specific ground — which is why there are two dims, not one.

The pairing rule

  • ✓--b-bg pairs with --b-onbg. --b-card pairs with --b-ink. Take the text colour from the same set as the surface behind it.
  • ✕Never mix the pairs. A container on --b-bg that inherits --b-ink renders black on black — present, selectable, invisible. This shipped once and nobody noticed for weeks.
  • ✓Cards stay light in both themes. Dark mode flips the page ground and the shadow, not the cards.
  • ✕Acid on white, or acid as a text colour on anything. It is a ground, and its ink is always #0a0a0a.

05 · Form

Printed, not rendered.

Keyline

3px solid --b-line on cards and controls, 2px on chips. Hairlines belong to other people's brands.

Shadow

Hard offset, zero blur. 6px 6px 0 on cards, 3–4px on buttons. In dark mode the shadow becomes acid.

Radius

None. Every corner in the --b-* system is square. Rounding one corner unpicks the whole language.

Press

A control moves toward its shadow on hover — translate 2px on both axes, shadow shrinks to match. The object appears to be pressed into the page. It is the only motion in the system, and it never animates on load.

06 · Voice

Say the thing, then stop.

  • ✓“We find the evidence. You say what it means.”
  • ✓Name the limitation in the same breath as the claim. Honesty about what a source can’t tell you is the product.
  • ✕Never write the take. The machine does evidence; a person does meaning. A question with an opinion hidden in it is still an opinion.
  • ✕No exclamation marks, no “seamless”, no “powerful”, no em-dash-joined marketing triplets.
